# Proposal

## Why

AUD-TST-08 (sprint 8) reports that the presence ratchet `testsMinimum` is dominated by combinatorics: a large share of
the executions it counts come from 13 test factories in three files. Re-read on `develop` at `8b26d3d7` on 2026-10-13,
the finding **holds**:

- 13 `@TestFactory` methods, all in three files of `morpheus-cli`: `BlankOptionValueRefusalTest`,
  `DuplicateOptionRefusalTest`, `NothingIsCreatedBeforeTheOptionsAreAcceptedTest`. No `@ParameterizedTest` and no
  `@RepeatedTest` anywhere in the repository, so those factories are the only source of executions that are not one
  declared method each.
- A text scan finds 2 429 `@Test` annotations in 518 files (the audit: 2 286), against `testsMinimum=3820` in
  `config/m21-quality-ratchets.properties`. A scan of annotations is not a count of executions and no margin is derived
  from it; it shows the order of magnitude: well over a thousand of the executions the ratchet requires are generated
  by the factories.
- The validators count what Surefire reports, without distinction: `validate-m21.sh:44-60` sums the `tests` attribute
  of every `TEST-*.xml`.
- The margin of the ratchet is small by design — `.claude/rules/testing.md` records ten executions above it at the last
  qualification. One factory multiplies every command by every option by every blank value: removing one option from
  one command removes more executions than the margin holds.

The factories are good tests: a real option matrix, a refusal before any write. The defect is in the measure. One
number answers two questions — were tests removed, and did the option matrix change — so removing a dead option breaks
the build as if tests had been deleted, and adding an option raises the count as if tests had been written.

## What Changes

- The validators and their evidence report two counts: executions of declared test methods, and executions generated by
  a factory.
- Each count has its own minimum in the living ratchet file. A change of the option matrix is refused or accepted under
  the generated minimum, by name, and never moves the written one.
- A factory that generates nothing fails, so the generated count cannot fall to zero unnoticed behind a passing
  written count.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

(none)

### Modified Capabilities

- `gate-verdict-currency`: requirements added — the two counts, their two minimums read from the living file, and the
  refusal of an empty factory. The existing requirement on reading the minimums from the living file is unchanged.

## Impact

- `config/m21-quality-ratchets.properties` (a governance file: one key added, `testsMinimum` re-based once on the
  written count — see the design, decision 3, and open question 1).
- `scripts/validate-m21.*`, `scripts/validate-d2.*`, `scripts/lib/` readers, their tests under `scripts/tests/`.
- `D2RepositoryHardeningArchitectureTest`, `ProductionIntegrityContractTest`, `RepositoryDocumentationCoherenceTest`
  where they pin a key or a value; every page that states the presence minimums (`rules/meta.md` lists the destinations
  found so far; the two coherence tests name the real ones).
- An ADR amendment records that the presence ratchet now has two measures and that the re-basing is not a lowering
  (ADR-0089, which carries the M21 quality ratchets; the skill `adr-author` confirms the home).
- `.claude/rules/testing.md`, `.claude/rules/governance.md`, the skill `live-numbers`.
- Better after `test-the-validators-that-give-a-verdict`, whose fabricated checkout is where the new refusals are
  provoked; not blocked by it.
